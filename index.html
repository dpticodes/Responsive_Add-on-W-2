About me

Hi! My name is Deepti and I live in Berlin, Germany. I love Shreya's smooth, crystal-clear, and nightingale-like voice. This page was created as part of a project for SheCodes Responsive to develop my coding skills and is open-sourced on GitHub.

About this website

This page showcases a selection of my favorite songs by Shreya Ghoshal. Grounded in a strong foundation of Indian classical music, her singing is defined by perfect pitch, seamless breath control, and effortless mastery over high notes. Whether capturing deep sorrow, vibrant joy, or romantic tenderness, she brings every emotion to life with effortless grace.
